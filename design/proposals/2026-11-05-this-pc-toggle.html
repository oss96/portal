Where the This PC switch lives

The This PC pane opens on the left of the server, but the button that opens it sits at the far right of the command bar, next to Details. These three options move the control to where the pane appears. All three drop the This PC button from the command bar. Hidden files, Details and Ctrl+K stay where they are, and Details still sits right above the pane it controls.
